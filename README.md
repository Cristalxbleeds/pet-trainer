### Good Pet Bell :3

Welcome to **Good Pet Bell** (https://pettrainer.vercel.app), a browser-based remote clicker and bell app designed for interactive clicker training and custom sound playback with no setup required :3

#### Quick Guide :3

- **Pet (Sound Receiver):** Select **I am the Pet (Sound Receiver)** at https://pettrainer.vercel.app, use the default dog clicker sound (`goodpet.mp3`) or upload a custom audio file, enter a room code, and press **Enter** (or click **Start Listening**) :3 Remember to click **Test Sound Locally** first to enable browser audio :3
- **Owner (Bell Clicker):** Select **I am the Owner (Bell Clicker)**, enter the shared room code, and press **Enter** (or click **Connect to Pet**) :3 Tap the **RING FOR GOOD PET** button or use your assigned keybind to ring the bell in real time :3
- **Custom Keybinds:** Assign any keyboard key (default: `Space`) or mouse button (Left, Middle, Right, M4, M5) on the Owner screen, which saves automatically and triggers when the browser tab is active :3
- **Private Rooms:** Support for multiple independent, private rooms simultaneously :3
